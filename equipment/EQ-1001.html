P-12 centrifugal pump — monthly service sheet

Round order

the assistant asks in exactly this order

  1. Visual. Damage to the housing, cables and connectors — before starting.
  2. Operation. Starts and runs steady: no unusual noise, vibration or smell.
  3. Readings — 3. Check each against the range below and say it out loud.
  4. Service — 4 steps. The list below, in order.
  5. Known issue. This unit has its own — check it separately.
  6. Safety. Guards in place, the emergency stop trips.
  7. Work done. What was replaced, cleaned or adjusted.

Say the figure out loud. A reading you did not speak cannot be recorded — the server checks the number against your own words. And answer as you go: a turn that saves them all for the end is sent back.

What to check, and how

ranges from the service sheet — the server judges against them

discharge pressure4.2–4.8 bar
Gauge on the discharge nozzle, right after the volute. Read it with the pump running, no sooner than two minutes after start — the needle jumps on start-up.
Out of range: Climbing past 5 bar is the seal flush line, not the impeller.
bearing temperature≤ 70 °C
Infrared thermometer on the bearing housing metal, coupling side. Not on the guard — the guard always reads cooler.
Out of range: Above 70 C stop the pump and report it; do not wait for the end of the round.
vibration at the bearing housing≤ 4.5 mm/s
Vibration meter held vertically on the bearing housing, probe pressed against clean metal.

Service steps

this round, in order

  • inspect the mechanical seal for weeping
  • grease both bearings with two strokes of EP2
  • confirm the coupling guard is bolted
  • verify the local emergency stop trips the motor

Known issues

what to expect from this unit in particular

  • the seal flush line clogs — a slow pressure climb above 5 bar usually means the flush line, not the impeller

What to do: Pressure drop or weeping — check the seal flush line first. Look at the impeller last.

Service sheet, verbatim

the same text the assistant reads

Normal operating range

Discharge pressure 4.2 to 4.8 bar, bearing temperature below 70 C, no visible shaft seal leakage. Vibration at the bearing housing below 4.5 mm/s.

Monthly service

Inspect the mechanical seal for weeping, grease both bearings with two strokes of EP2, confirm the coupling guard is bolted, verify the local emergency stop trips the motor.

Known issues

Seal flush line clogs on this unit; a slow pressure climb above 5 bar usually means the flush line, not the impeller.
