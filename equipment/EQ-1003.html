Normal operating range

Supply air 16 to 20 C in cooling mode, filter differential below 150 Pa, belt deflection 10 to 15 mm at mid-span, no water carry-over past the coil.

Quarterly service

Replace bag filters, check belt wear and tension, flush the condensate tray and trap, verify damper actuators travel fully open and closed, confirm the fire damper link is intact.

Known issues

The condensate trap dries out over winter and lets air bypass; refill the trap before condemning the drain line.
