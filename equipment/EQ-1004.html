Normal operating range

Output 48 V nominal, charge current tapering below 30 A at end of cycle, cabinet fans running whenever charging, connector pins bright with no pitting.

Monthly service

Vacuum the cabinet vents, inspect the DC connector and cable strain relief, confirm the wall-mounted emergency cutoff isolates the charger, check the floor marking around the bay is legible.

Known issues

Pitted connector pins on this unit have twice caused a no-charge complaint logged against the battery; check the pins first.
