Normal operating range

Working pressure 7.0 to 7.5 bar, oil temperature 75 to 95 C, condensate drain cycling automatically. Intake filter differential below 0.05 bar.

Weekly service

Drain condensate manually and confirm the auto drain fires, check oil level in the sight glass at half, wipe and inspect the intake filter, listen for unloader chatter at idle.

Known issues

The auto drain valve on this unit sticks in cold weather; a puddle under bay 2 means the drain, check it before suspecting the separator.
