  first_start — First start — 8 steps
    1 plug_in: Plug the charger from the box into either USB-C port on the top edge.
    2 power_on: Press the power button on the top edge once. The stick rings light up while it starts.
    3 wait_screen: Give it a minute for the screen, and about two for Windows to load.
    4 region_keyboard: Pick your country or region, then your keyboard layout.
    5 wifi: Connect to a Wi-Fi network.
    6 updates: Now it checks for updates, about three minutes, then restarts by itself.
    7 license_account: Accept the MSI licence, set your preferences, and wait a couple of minutes.
    8 msi_center_default: Restart it and choose MSI Center M as the start screen when it asks.
  power_led — Power button and status light — 3 cards
    1 power_use: Press to turn on or sleep. Hold it to shut down.
    2 led_states: White is on, amber is charging, blinking amber is a battery fault.
    3 fingerprint: The power button doubles as the fingerprint reader.
  charging — Charging and battery — 4 cards
    1 charge_it: Plug the charger into either USB-C port. The light goes amber while it charges.
    2 when_full: It stops at full and won't top up until it drops a little. Unplug it once it's full.
    3 power_bank: A strong enough USB-C power bank can charge it too.
    4 battery_care: No need to drain it first, but a full run-down once a month helps.
  msi_center — MSI Center M — 3 cards

    3 msi_mode_controls: Use the D-pad to move, A to select and B to go back.

    4 macro_buttons: M1 and M2 on the back can be set to anything, including a recorded macro.
  quick_settings — Quick Settings — 2 cards

  sign_in — PIN and fingerprint — 5 steps
